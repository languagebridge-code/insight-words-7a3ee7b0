What We Collect

From Students: Almost Nothing

  • Text for translation: When you select text, we send it to Microsoft Azure for translation. We don't store it.
  • Language preference: We remember which language you chose (stored in your browser).
  • Usage stats: Anonymous counts like "150 translations today" (no names, no personal info).

We do NOT collect:

✗ Names
✗ Email addresses
✗ Student IDs
✗ Grades
✗ Browsing history
✗ Personal information of any kind

From Schools

When schools purchase LanguageBridge:

  • School/district name
  • Administrator contact info
  • Number of licenses
  • Aggregate usage data (not linked to individual students)

Technical Stuff

  • Browser type (for compatibility)
  • Extension version
  • Error logs (no personal info)
  • Language pairs used (aggregated across all users)

How It Works

Translation Process

  1. You select text
  2. Text is sent to Microsoft Azure via encrypted HTTPS
  3. Azure returns the translation
  4. LanguageBridge does not store the text or the translation
  5. You hear or see the translation

LanguageBridge does not retain translation text. Microsoft Azure may retain limited transient logs strictly for abuse prevention, security, and service reliability under its enterprise privacy commitments, and does not use customer data to train its translation models.

Google Docs & Clipboard

Why we need clipboard permission: Google Docs blocks normal text selection. When you copy text in Google Docs, we read it from your clipboard to translate it.

What we do NOT do:

  • Monitor your clipboard continuously
  • Read clipboard data outside Google Docs contexts
  • Store anything you copy

Text-to-Speech

Same as translation: text is sent to Azure Speech Services over encrypted HTTPS and synthesized audio is returned. LanguageBridge does not store the text or the audio. Audio is played in your browser and discarded.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

LanguageBridge is offered to schools as a classroom tool and may be used by students under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the FTC's COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312).

Data Minimization:

✓ No student account, login, name, email, or persistent identifier is collected by LanguageBridge
✓ No behavioral profiles, advertising IDs, or cross-site tracking
✓ Translation text is processed to deliver the service and is not retained by LanguageBridge
✓ Student data is never used for marketing, advertising, or to build student profiles for non-educational purposes

School Authorization (COPPA School Exception):

LanguageBridge is provided to K-12 schools and districts for use solely in a school-authorized educational context. Consistent with the FTC's longstanding guidance on COPPA and schools, the school or district may provide consent on behalf of parents for the limited collection of student information needed to deliver the service, in lieu of obtaining direct parental consent, when:

  • Use is limited to the educational context the school has authorized;
  • Any information collected is used only to provide the requested educational service;
  • Information is not used or disclosed for any commercial purpose unrelated to the school-authorized service; and
  • Parents are given notice of the school's use of the service through the district's standard parent notification practices.

Schools and districts are responsible for providing required notices to parents and for managing parent opt-outs in accordance with their local policies and applicable law.

No Direct Marketing to Children: LanguageBridge does not display advertising, market to students, or use any student information to contact a child directly.

Student Rights (FERPA Compliance)

LanguageBridge complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA):

No Education Records Created:

  • We do not create, store, or maintain education records
  • No student performance data is collected
  • No grades or assessment data is tracked

Legitimate Educational Interest:

  • Translation and reading support serve a legitimate educational purpose
  • Real-time processing means no records are created

Microsoft Azure as School Official:

  • Microsoft Azure acts as a "school official" under FERPA
  • Azure has a legitimate educational interest in providing translation services
  • Azure is under direct control of the school through their enterprise agreement
  • Microsoft Azure's FERPA compliance: https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/regulatory/offering-ferpa

Ohio Senate Bill 29 (Ohio Rev. Code § 3319.325) Compliance

✓ Aligned with Ohio Rev. Code § 3319.325

Ohio Senate Bill 29, codified at Ohio Revised Code § 3319.325, governs how K-12 school districts and their technology providers handle student data. LanguageBridge is designed to align with the requirements of ORC § 3319.325; our privacy-first architecture goes beyond the statutory minimum because we do not store educational records, track students, or collect student personally identifiable information.

Breach Notification Commitment

Consistent with Ohio Rev. Code § 3319.325 and our contractual obligations to school districts, LanguageBridge maintains a written security incident response plan and commits to the following:

✓ 30-day breach notification: Affected school districts will be notified, in writing, within 30 days of LanguageBridge's confirmation of a security breach involving district or student data, or sooner where required by law or contract.
✓ Notifications include the nature and timing of the incident, categories of data involved, containment steps, and recommended actions.
✓ Dedicated security contact: privacy@languagebridge.app
✓ Written incident response procedures documented internally and reviewed periodically.

Note: Because LanguageBridge does not store student PII or education records, the volume of student information potentially exposed by an incident affecting our systems is structurally limited. Districts remain responsible for breach response involving data they store in their own systems.

For Schools

School districts may authorize LanguageBridge for classroom use under FERPA's "school official" exception (34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)) and, for students under 13, may provide consent on behalf of parents under the COPPA school-authorization framework, as described in the Children's Privacy section above. The service is used solely for the educational purposes the district authorizes.

A written Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and SB 29 contract addendum are available for any school district that requests one at privacy@languagebridge.app.

International Use

LanguageBridge is based in the United States. Microsoft Azure may process translation requests in data centers in multiple regions under its enterprise privacy and security commitments. Schools outside the U.S. should review their local data protection laws before deploying the service.

For school districts and institutions in the EU, UK, and other jurisdictions with GDPR-equivalent requirements, LanguageBridge will enter into a written Data Processing Agreement (DPA) covering the limited processing activities required to deliver the service, including standard contractual clauses where applicable. Districts can request a DPA at privacy@languagebridge.app.
